Matteo Pagliero

pagliero [at] imada.sdu.dk

Since September 2025 I am a postdoc in the Operator Algebras group at the University of Southern Denmark (SDU), in Odense. I obtained my PhD in 2025 from KU Leuven, where my research was supported by a PhD-grant from the Research Foundation Flanders (FWO).

My primary focus is the structure and classification of C*-algebras and their dynamical analogue. However, I am also interested in many other problems concerning operator algebras. Scroll down to see my publications and preprints.

I organise the SDU Operator Algebras Seminar
